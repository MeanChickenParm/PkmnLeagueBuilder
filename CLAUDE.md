# Pokémon League Builder: how to work on this repo

Pokémon League Builder is a fork of Pokémon League GM (`MeanChickenParm/PokemonLeagueGM`, branch `v2`, version 2.0.2, commit `9ae0fb8`) turned inside out: the player runs a League and tries to let no challenger through. The design is the Project doc `claude/game-design.md`. Read it before building anything.

The two games never merge. A battle fix made in one has to be copied to the other by hand, so keep changes to the battle code in `game/3-engine-1.js` small and easy to find.

This repo is the only master copy of the game. Every chat works the same way: clone, build, change, test, push.

## Working rules (agreed Nov 12, 2026)
- The GitHub repo is the only master copy. Never build from a file in the claude.ai Project, an attachment, or an earlier chat's workspace.
- Edit the source pieces in `game/`, never the built HTML.
- One chat = one new version number, with all of that chat's changes in one changelog entry (date and Eastern Time timestamp).
- Test before every push: the Node sim, the headless browser play-through and the two code checks below must all pass.
- **Don't send the game files unless asked.** The build still makes the game HTML and the itch.io zip and they are checked every chat, but they are only sent to the owner when the owner asks for them, once the game is playable. The built game is not committed to the repo (there is no GitHub Pages site yet).
- Keep this file up to date, and keep the design doc in the Project up to date with what was decided and what was built.

## Where the build stands
- **0.1.0 (the seed):** the source with the cut systems removed. The world runs on its own; the player controls nothing yet.
- **0.2.0 (stage 1a, the wall rules):** set-level battles everywhere, challengers without levels, three tries per gym, two gym trainers per gym, the Champion keeps the seat, and the wall report. Staff are still placed automatically and nothing is tuned.
- **0.3.0 (stage 1b-1, first half):** gym types from the leader's class (dual types kept, untyped leaders allowed), gym trainers that share a type with the leader, a staff team builder that reads Scouting, Strategy and Training, teams that stay on type, the meta response and the performance-based staff moves switched off, and a seeded wall report with staff tiers. Not tuned: tuning waited for the continuous-world calendar.
- **0.4.0 (stage 1b-1, second half):** the continuous world. No seasons: a 48-week year that never stops, challengers on their own clocks (runs and rests), the year as the unit the wall is scored on, slow changes spread through the year by birthday, no cap on a circuit, sim controls that stop when something needs the player, and the save, history, records and interface brought in line. Tuning was tried and stopped: the four agreed dials barely move the wall (see Balance below).
- **0.5.0 (stage 1b-1, tuning):** the wall tuned with one new lever, `BUILD_PAR` (every staff team is built as if its trainer's Scouting, Strategy and Training were 45 points lower). The Elite Four stays one run. An older, larger field (give-up after three years, at 0.2), challengers who reached the Elite Four moving on three times in four, and the wall report leading with trainers let through.
- **0.6.0 (stage 1b-2, first piece):** founding by hand. The player places the 13 senior posts of their League from a founding pool (about 40 candidates, some famous) and the founding field (now 36); gym trainers are taken from the circuit; everyone not placed starts on the circuit. Staff step down only after a long career, announced a year ahead. A vacant Gym Leader's or Elite Four seat in the player's League waits for the player with an acting holder. Testing slimmed down: the wall report runs only on request.
- **Next:** see the design doc, section 12.

## How the world is stored (things to know before changing it)
- `S` is the whole save. `SAVE_VERSION` (in `3-engine-1.js`) is 3 (bump it whenever a change makes old saves unusable; a save with another number is not loaded); the save key is `plb-save`. Every storage key starts with `plb-` or `pokemon-league-builder-`. This game never reads a Pokémon League GM save.
- **The calendar (0.4.0): one continuous world, no seasons and no phases.** `S.phase` is always `'season'` (kept so old checks stay true). A year is `YEAR_WEEKS` (48) weekly turns, twelve months of four. **`S.season` is the year's number** (the field name is kept from the source, as are `seasonHist`, `titleSeasons`, `t.seasons` and so on: they all count years now), `S.week` is the week of the year (0 to 47) and `S.turn` the weeks since the world began. Every timer that can cross New Year (rests, legendary, roamer, storm, wormhole and outbreak timers, gym adapt cooldown) uses `S.turn`. `dateName()`, `weekName(w)`, `weekShort(w)` and `turnDate(turn)` put dates in words.
  - `simWeek()` is one week of the whole world: every League's `leagueWeek()`, then `birthdays()`, `arrivals()`, the clock, and `newYear()` when week 48 is reached. `simYear()` plays to the next New Year and returns that year's history entry; `simWeeks(n, stops)` plays up to n weeks and, with `stops`, returns early with `S.stop` (`{ why: 'title' | 'vacancy' | 'newyear', text }`, set by `stopFor`).
  - **A challenger's clock:** `t.rest` is the turn they set out again (null while out on a run); `t.runStart` the turn the run began; `t.runs` their career runs; `t.last` how the last run ended; `t.ready` weeks spent preparing with eight badges; `t.early` runs in a row that ended early; `t.leaving` a retirement decided on their birthday that waits for the run to end. `setOut(t)` starts a run (no badges, fresh tries, best `START_TEAM` Pokémon); `endRun(t, entry, stage)` ends it (stage null = out on a third loss), decides giving up and League moves (`leagueMove`), and sets the rest (`REST_WEEKS` 6 to 10, +2 from age 40; `REST_QUICK` 2 to 3 for a challenger who reached the Elite Four and moves to another League, which they do `TOUR_CHANCE` (0.75) of the time when there is one). `t.out` is gone.
  - **The Elite Four:** with eight badges `aiActionBase` returns `'league'` once the team has six Pokémon or `READY_WEEKS` (3) weeks have passed, and `leagueRun(t, entry)` plays the Elite Four and Champion at once. There is no League tournament: `leagueFinals`, `runLeague`, `nextSeason`, `autoStep`, `startSeason` and `simOneSeason` are gone.
  - **The year's record:** `yearLeague()` gives `S.league`, the bound League's running record of the year (`entries` League runs, `logs` title battles kept for replay, `conceded`, `out`, `runs`, `best`, `bestId`); `S.lastLeague` is last year's, shown by the year in review. `yearOf(t)` is a trainer's own tally of the year (`b` most badges in a run, `st` best League stage, `pts`, `runs`); `trainerPoints(t)` reads it. `t.weekLog` is now the year's log (cleared at New Year), not a run's.
  - **Birthdays:** `bweek(t)` is a trainer's birthday week (from their id). `birthdays()` ages them, moves their ratings and decides retirement: a challenger out on a run gets `t.leaving`, staff may announce they are stepping down and leave a year later through `staffRetires(t)` (0.6.0, see Stepping down and vacancies).
  - **New Year** (`newYear()`, `leagueNewYear()`): the wall report into `S.history`, each challenger's `seasonHist` entry (their best of the year), awards, the gyms' year, the meta report, the ban ballot and vote, the World Championship (2+ Leagues), `worldGrowth()`. `S.changes` and `S.progress` hold last year's changes and rating moves (collected through the year in `S.yearChanges` by `noteChange`, and `S.yearProgress`).
  - **World events** start in any week at the source's yearly rates (`EVENT_PACE`, `stormAt`) and leave on their own timers.
- **Leagues:** the bound League's state sits directly on `S` (`S.gyms`, `S.e4`, `S.champion`, `S.challengers`…); the others wait in `S.leagues`. Use `inLeague(i, fn)` / `eachLeague(fn)`; `LEAGUE_FIELDS` lists what belongs to a League. Each League has a `region` (an index into `LEAGUE_SETS`).
- **Cities are world objects**, not part of a League: `S.cities[id] = { id, name, region }`, made by `regionCities(region)` the first time a League is founded in a region. A gym sits in a city: `gym.city` is the city's id. `gymCity(i)` and `townName(i)` give the bound League's gym `i`. Two Leagues founded in the same region share the same city objects.
- **Founding by hand (0.6.0, `5-engine-3.js`):** `newWorld({ hand: true })` (the setup screen always passes it; tests and the report don't) sets `S.cfg.hand` and, for League 0, calls `prepFounding()` instead of `foundLeague()`: empty gyms in the region's cities, and `S.founding = { cands, field, champion, e4[4], gyms[8] }`, where `cands` are about 40 founding candidates (`FOUND_PER_POST` 3 per senior post at the strengths `foundLeague` uses, `FOUND_STARS` 3 at 76 to 82, 4 to 6 of them famous) and `field` the League's founding field. All are role `free` and on no circuit until the League opens. `foundPlace(post, slot, id)` ('champion', 'e4' 0-3, 'gym' 0-7; id null clears), `foundPool()`, `foundPlaced()`, `foundTypesUsed()` (repeated types are allowed and only flagged), `foundingFill()` (best available by OVR, Champion, then Elite Four seat 4 down, then gyms 8 down, −6 for a type already held) and `finishFounding()` (fills what's left, installs, puts everyone else on League 0's circuit, `staffGym` each gym, founds the other Leagues' fields, `startWorld()`). The world is saved mid-founding; the page shows `viewFounding()` while `S.founding` exists. `handOn()` and `playerLeague()` (League 0 bound, hand on, not the report's fixed tiers) tell code it is the player's League. Rival Leagues and every League opened later are founded automatically, as before. `foundingField(lgs)` now takes the Leagues to fill.
- **Gym trainers from the circuit (0.6.0):** `staffGym` fills an empty place with `fillGymTrainer`, which takes the circuit's best fitting challenger (`circuitGymTrainer`: 16+, not leaving, not on an Elite Four run, no title won) through `takeGymTrainer` (keeps up to two on-type Pokémon, the rest built by `staffPicks`), and makes one only when nobody fits. Not in the report's fixed tiers (`fixedStaff()`).
- **Stepping down and vacancies (0.6.0, `4-engine-2.js`):** staff no longer retire by `ageRetireChance`. On a birthday, `stepDownChance(t)` is 0 unless `t.seasons >= careerSpan(t)` and `postYears(t) >= STEP_DOWN_YEARS` (10); then `announceStepDown` sets `t.stepDown` (a year ahead), with news and a `'stepdown'` stop, and on the next birthday `staffRetires(t)` runs. In the player's League (`playerLeague()`) a leaving Gym Leader is replaced by an acting leader (`actingLeader`: the gym's stronger gym trainer, role `leader`, `t.acting` and `gym.acting`; their gym trainer place stays empty and `staffGym` keeps the gym exactly one short, letting nobody go), an Elite Four member by a stand-in (`actingE4`, `t.acting`), and a Champion by the strongest non-acting Elite Four member at once, whose seat then gets a stand-in. Each opens `S.vacancies` (world-level: `{ post: 'leader'|'e4', slot, since, outsiders }`, outsiders are two role-`prospect` trainers). `vacancyCands(v)` (circuit, the League's gym trainers, the acting leader, the outsiders, best first), `fillVacancy(v, id)` (the acting leader goes back to their gym trainer place; a stand-in retires; unpicked outsiders join the circuit), `autoFillVacancy`, `fillAllVacancies` (the multi-year sim calls it). Other Leagues still fill at once (`fillGym`, `outsiderE4`). `stopFor` ignores other Leagues when hand is on.
- **Trainers:** `S.trainers[id]`, with `role` one of `challenger`, `leader`, `gymtrainer`, `e4`, `champion`, `free`, `prospect`, `retired` (`exchamp` exists only for a moment while a Champion retires). `STAFF_ROLES` is the four post roles; `staffIds()` lists everyone holding a post in the bound League. There are no teams: a challenger is on one League's circuit (`S.challengers`). **A circuit has no cap** (0.4.0); `CIRCUIT_SIZE` (24) is only the size of a founding field.
- **Newcomers and the field:** `arrivals()` brings `NEWCOMERS_PER_LEAGUE` (8) new trainers a year per League, one every six weeks, and puts any free agent on the emptiest circuit (`fillCircuits`). Nobody is pushed out. The field is kept in check by weak challengers giving up in `endRun`: `GIVE_UP_RUNS` (3) runs in a row that ended on three losses no deeper than a typical run (`S.runAvg`, a running average), after `GIVE_UP_GRACE` (3) years, with chance `GIVE_UP_CHANCE` (0.2) at each further early run (0.5.0, the owner chose an older field). That holds the field at about 50 to 75 in a one-League world, a little more per League with several.
- **The wall rules (0.2.0):**
  - Levels and sizes are constants in `3-engine-1.js`: `GYM_LV`, `GYM_SIZE` (3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6), `E4_LV`, `E4_SIZE` (6), `CHAMP_LV`, `GYM_TRAINERS` (2), `GYM_TRIES` (3).
  - Every circuit battle goes through `setBattle(t, foe, lvl, stakes, title)`: it picks the challenger's squad with `pickSquad` (as many Pokémon as the foe fields, by matchup, with noise from a low Strategy rating) and calls `battle()` with `opts.equal = lvl`. Staff teams still carry levels from `relevelElite`, but they don't matter in a set-level battle.
  - **Challengers have no levels.** `setTeamLevel(t, L)` puts every Pokémon at level `L` in the form it would have there (`stageAt`; `m.top` remembers the furthest form reached, so a line can go back down to its first form and up again along the same branch) with that level's moves. It runs when a run starts, after every weekly action and before each League battle; `challengerLevel(t)` is the level of the next battle. There is no level gain: `gainLevels`, `PACE` and the Lucky Egg are gone. The weekly actions left are challenge, explore (catch), train (friendship only) and seek (a legendary).
  - **Gym tries:** `gymTry()` in `4-engine-2.js`. A challenger carries `t.gymTries` (losses at the current gym) and `t.gymStage` (gym trainers already beaten there); the third loss calls `endRun`. A gym's `w` is tries turned back and `l` badges conceded this year (reset at New Year; `gym.hist` keeps each year).
  - **Gym trainers:** `gym.trainers` holds two trainer ids with role `gymtrainer`. `staffGym(i)` fills the gym and lets go of any who no longer fit; it runs at founding, every New Year and whenever a gym's staff change. `gymFits(t, leader)`: an untyped leader takes any gym trainer, a typed one needs at least one shared type. Each gym trainer fields their own class's types.
  - **Types (0.3.0):** a post's type is its holder's class. `specTypes(t)` gives 0, 1 or 2 types; `typeLabel(t)` puts them in words ('Rock/Flying', 'mixed'). Nothing forces a type on a trainer who takes a post any more, and a dual-type leader keeps `spec2`. `gymWeatherType(leader)` is the first of the leader's types that brings weather; `gymTry` passes it to `setBattle` so gym trainers fight in it too.
  - **The staff team builder (0.3.0, `3-engine-1.js`):** `staffPicks(t, L, n, { lines, team, mixed })` replaces `buildEliteTeam`. `staffPool` lists on-type species under a Scouting ceiling (`SCOUT_BST`) and keeps the strongest `scoutShare(t)` of them; picks are scored by `fitScore` (base stats, stacked weaknesses, answers to the types that beat the trainer's type) with noise from low Strategy (`STRAT_NOISE`); `staffMon` sets IVs from Training (`TRAIN_IV`) and, as often as `trainCare(t)`, a fitting nature and the coverage that answers the Pokémon's own weaknesses. A trainer with 50s builds about what the old builder did. `eliteItems` gives a gym's staff one more held item at Strategy 75+, one fewer under 45. `relevelElite` still sets levels, which don't count in a set-level battle.
  - **Switched off in 0.3.0:** `AUTO_STAFF_MOVES` (false) gates the source's performance moves (a leader replaced after three poor seasons, a leader's comeback to the circuit, a challenger taking an Elite Four seat after a deep run). The meta report still runs, but staff no longer add counters for it. Filling a vacancy (a challenger or an outsider takes a gym; the strongest Elite Four member moves up) still happens.
  - **Tuning lever (0.5.0):** `BUILD_PAR` (95) in `3-engine-1.js`: the rating at which a trainer builds an ordinary team. `bld(t, k)` is the rating the builder reads (`t.r[k] - (BUILD_PAR - 50)`); `staffPool`, `scoutShare`, `staffPicks`, `staffMon` and `trainCare` use it. Battle ratings (Bond, Nerve, Strategy inside the battle) and `eliteItems` read the real ratings. This is the dial to move if the wall needs retuning; the four agreed dials barely move it (see Balance).
  - **Titles:** `leagueRun()` gives a challenger with eight badges one run. One who beats the Champion gets a title and stays a challenger; `S.league.conceded` lists them and `S.lastTitle` is the turn of the last one (the header's "since a title was conceded"). Each `S.history` entry is one year: `titles`, `badges`, `best` (0 to 8 badges, 9 to 12 Elite Four members beaten, 13 the title; `depthName()` puts it in words), `bestId`, `runs` (runs begun), `qualifiers` (runs that reached the Elite Four), `out` (runs ended on three losses), `field` (challengers at New Year) and `winners`. **A year is perfect when `titles` is 0.**
  - The Champion leaves only by stepping down after a long career, in `staffRetires()`; the strongest Elite Four member moves up.
- **Randomness:** every draw goes through `rand()`. `seedRandom(n)` makes a run repeatable; `seedRandom()` goes back to `Math.random`. The game itself is never seeded.
- **Staff tiers (tests only):** `newWorld({ staffBoost: N })` makes every staff member the game creates N rating points better or worse (`staffQ`), and stops challengers walking into posts (`fixedStaff()`), so a run stays in its tier.
- `BATTLE_RULE` in the battle engine still supports special rules (inverse, scalemons, flipped, noitems, trickroom) but nothing sets it. `battle()` takes `opts.equal` for same-level battles.

## Start of every chat
1. Clone the repo (or `git pull` if it's already cloned).
2. `python3 game/build.py game dist`. Note the version it prints: that's the current version.
3. Read the design doc in the Project and `TODO.md`.

## Making changes
- Edit the pieces in `game/`, never the built HTML.
- New Pokémon, moves and abilities go in `game/2-pokemon-data.js`.
- One chat = one new version: add one entry at the top of `UPDATES` in `game/8-ui-3.js` with the next whole-number `id`, the next `ver` (`0.1.0`, `0.2.0`…: the build names its files from it), the date, and an Eastern Time timestamp. All changes in the chat go into that one entry, written for players.
- The changelog starts at 0.1.0. Don't copy Pokémon League GM's history into it.

## Testing (before every push)
Run all of these after `python3 game/build.py game dist`. Each must pass.
- `node tests/sim.js <leagues> <growth 0/1> <years> <god 0/1>` sims years week by week and checks the world after every week: posts filled, every gym in a city of its League's region, one circuit per challenger, no state from the cut systems, and a save that reloads and keeps running. It also checks the wall rules (team sizes, two gym trainers of the leader's type at every gym, tries, challengers' teams at the set level) and the calendar (the turn matches the year and week, every challenger is either resting with a date or out on a run, one history entry per year, the field between 8 and 120, a sim with stops halts for New Year). It prints `ok` and the balance numbers (Elite Four runs, perfect years and badges conceded a year, runs a year, the field). Before every push run `1 0 12 0`, `1 1 20 1`, `3 1 12 1` and the hand-placed `1 0 12 0 1` (a fifth argument `1` founds League 0 by best available, forces a Gym Leader, an Elite Four member and the Champion to leave in year 2, fills vacancies now and then, and checks acting holders).
- `python3 tests/browser.py "$PWD/dist/Pokémon League Builder.html"` plays three years through the real page in a headless browser with the Next week, Next month and To New Year buttons, opening every view at several points of each year, and reloads from the save. Run it on `dist/itch/index.html` too. It must end with `FAILS 0 ERRORS 0`.
- `node tests/report.js [years] [worlds] [--seed=N] [--boost=N] [--tiers]` is the wall report (not a pass/fail test): trainers let through (titles conceded, in total and a year, the headline since 0.5.0), perfect years in 100, titles and badges conceded a year, runs begun and runs that reached the Elite Four, how far the best challenger got, and how often each gym turned a try back. It is slow (a year is about a second now that the field is bigger): `100 4` takes about 8 minutes and `25 12 --tiers` about 20. It is seeded (seed 1 by default), so the same command prints the same numbers. `--tiers` prints one line each for strong (+15), average (0) and poor (-15) staff. Years in one world share its staff, so for a steady number use more worlds rather than more years. **Since 0.6.0 (the owner, Nov 13) the report is not run every chat:** run it only when the owner asks or the chat is about balance. The game is far from finished and balance can wait. It founds Leagues automatically, so it does not cover founding by hand.
- `python3 tests/unused.py` lists functions and click handlers nothing can reach. It should report `dead 0 lines 0`.
- `tests/lint.sh` runs ESLint over the joined source for names that are used but never defined. It should print only the browser globals it doesn't know (`indexedDB`, `sessionStorage`) and a few unused locals.

Balance at 0.5.0, tuned (1 League, seed 1; `25 12 --tiers` rechecked at the start of 0.6.0: strong unchanged to the digit; 0.6.0's larger founding field and gym trainers from the circuit have not been measured). `node tests/report.js 100 4`: 2560 trainers let through in 400 years (6.40 a year), 3.3 perfect years in 100, 443.5 badges conceded a year (2.25 a run), 196.9 runs begun by 63.7 challengers, 14.26 runs a year reaching the Elite Four. `node tests/report.js 25 12 --tiers`: strong (+15) 3.56 let through a year and 14.7 perfect years in 100; average (0) 10.90 and 2.7; poor (-15) 20.57 and 1.0. The owner's target (Nov 13): a perfect year is an extreme feat, about 3 to 4 let through a year for strong staff, with no exact figure. Tuned with `BUILD_PAR` alone (95); the Elite Four stays one run, by the owner's choice. What was tested before choosing (strong staff, 8 worlds of 15 years): the wild cap and keeping teams between runs did nothing; more runs (shorter rests, more newcomers, no giving up) did nothing; Elite Four tries helped only once more challengers got there; the builder was the only lever that moved the wall on its own (design doc 3.5 has the table). (0.4.0 for comparison: strong 92.0, average 67.7, poor 22.0 perfect years in 100.)

## End of every chat
1. `git pull` first. If someone pushed a newer version in the meantime, merge their changes in and give this chat's work the next version number; never overwrite theirs.
2. Rebuild and run every test.
3. Commit `game/`, `tests/` and the docs with a message naming the version (e.g. `v0.2.0: badge-level gym battles`), and push to `main`.
4. Update `claude/game-design.md` in the Project with what was decided and what was built, and say what the next chat should cover.
5. Send the game HTML and the itch.io zip only if the owner asked for them.
